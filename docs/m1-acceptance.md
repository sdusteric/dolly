# M1 acceptance

M1 counts as done when you approve one real exported demo (spec section 12, item 13). This takes
about ten minutes.

## Record

1. Pick one of the family apps you'd actually post about (Shelf's Tile Board, Snap, Reel, Booth).
2. Press ⌃⌥⌘R. Choose Window and pick the app's window, then Record.
3. Do a 30 to 60 second demo with 4 to 8 clicks on real controls, at your normal pace.
4. Press ⌃⌥⌘R to stop. The take opens in the editor with its zooms already placed.

## Edit only if you need to

- Play it once (Space) before you touch anything. The question below is about the automatic zooms.
- Then fix what bothers you: click a zoom and drag its spike corners on the stage, drag its ends on
  the timeline, press Delete to drop one, Z to add one at the playhead.

## Export

1. Press ⌘E, keep 16:9 and 60 fps, choose Export.
2. Choose Copy, paste the clip into a draft post on X or Threads, and watch it there at phone size.

bounced. The fix is a number in `ZoomRules` or `CameraPath`.
